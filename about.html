A note from our school

Every great journey starts with a first lesson.

At Kparteh’s Village Elementary and Junior High School, learning is a journey we make together. Our school brings children, families, teachers, and neighbours together around a shared belief: that every young person deserves room to grow.

We welcome curious minds and big questions. We celebrate effort, kindness, and the courage to try something new. And we know the strongest foundation for a child’s future is a community that believes in them.
